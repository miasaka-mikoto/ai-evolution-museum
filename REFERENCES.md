# References

Primary and historical references used to identify the ideas. The project does not bundle copyrighted papers, images, fonts, videos or model weights. Entries are grouped so every one of the 30 scenes has a documented anchor.

## Foundations, rules and search

- Turing, A. M. (1936), *On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem* — Turing Machine.
- Shannon, C. E. (1938), *A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits* — Boolean Logic Network.
- Kleene, S. C. (1956), *Representation of Events in Nerve Nets and Finite Automata* — finite-state-machine context.
- McCulloch, W. S. & Pitts, W. (1943), *A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity* — threshold neuron and FSM context.
- Turing, A. M. (1950), *Computing Machinery and Intelligence* — Imitation Game.
- von Neumann, J. (1928), *Zur Theorie der Gesellschaftsspiele* — minimax theorem.
- Shannon, C. E. (1950), *Programming a Computer for Playing Chess* — chess-search reconstruction.
- Dijkstra, E. W. (1959), *A note on two problems in connexion with graphs* — Dijkstra Search.
- Knuth, D. E. & Moore, R. W. (1975), *An Analysis of Alpha-Beta Pruning* — Alpha–Beta bounds.
- Hart, P. E., Nilsson, N. J. & Raphael, B. (1968), *A Formal Basis for the Heuristic Determination of Minimum Cost Paths* — A*.

## Learning, memory and symbolic systems

- Rosenblatt, F. (1958), *The Perceptron: A Probabilistic Model for Information Storage and Organization in the Brain* — Perceptron.
- Weizenbaum, J. (1966), *ELIZA — A Computer Program for the Study of Natural Language Communication Between Man and Machine* — ELIZA.
- Gardner, M. (1970), *Mathematical Games: The Fantastic Combinations of John Conway's New Solitaire Game of Life* — Conway's Game of Life.
- Buchanan, B. G. & Shortliffe, E. H. (1984), *Rule-Based Expert Systems* — Expert System reconstruction.
- Hopfield, J. J. (1982), *Neural Networks and Physical Systems with Emergent Collective Computational Abilities* — Hopfield Network.
- Rumelhart, D. E., Hinton, G. E. & Williams, R. J. (1986), *Learning Representations by Back-propagating Errors* — Backpropagation.
- Holland, J. H. (1975), *Adaptation in Natural and Artificial Systems* — Genetic Algorithm.
- Watkins, C. J. C. H. (1989), *Learning from Delayed Rewards* — Q-Learning.
- Rummery, G. A. & Niranjan, M. (1994), *On-Line Q-Learning Using Connectionist Systems* — SARSA.
- Kohonen, T. (1982), *Self-organized Formation of Topologically Correct Feature Maps* — SOM.
- Lloyd, S. (1982), *Least Squares Quantization in PCM* — K-Means formulation.
- Quinlan, J. R. (1986), *Induction of Decision Trees* — Decision Tree.
- Langton, C. G. (1989), *Artificial Life* — Cellular Evolution context.

- LeCun, Y. et al. (1989), *Backpropagation Applied to Handwritten Zip Code Recognition* — CNN context.
- Goodfellow, I. et al. (2014), *Generative Adversarial Nets* — 2D GAN.
- Sohl-Dickstein, J. et al. (2015), *Deep Unsupervised Learning using Nonequilibrium Thermodynamics* — Diffusion.
- Vaswani, A. et al. (2017), *Attention Is All You Need* — Transformer.
- Bengio, Y. et al. (2003), *A Neural Probabilistic Language Model* — Autoregressive language-model lineage.
- Ha, D. & Schmidhuber, J. (2018), *World Models* — Agent + World Model.
